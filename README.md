# Python-numpy-pandas-project

# Sea Asia GDP per Capita Rate of Change

## Objective
Using Pandas and matplotlib, explore and get GDP per Capita Rate of change of Southeast Asian countries in the past 5 years with Population and GDP data

## Key Findings

Timor-Leste has the most positive rate of change 39% (2020-2021), Singapore 2nd 23% (2020-2021)  
Timor-Leste and the most negative rate of change -31% (2022-2023), Laos 2nd 20% (2021-2022)  
SEA Asia had their best years at 2024-2025 having positive rate of change for all countries

## Tools Used
- Python, pandas, matplotlib
